Three other explanations were tested. Three machines spent too little time on the floor to earn a comparable total.

06

Model and validation

Model validation is a fixed May–Jun holdout trained on Jan–Apr, so it does not change with the period filter. No future data enters training.

The machine model predicts daily play intensity from zone, game, weekday and month. It estimates the effect of moving a machine, which informs the budget recommendations.

The floor model forecasts daily coin-in by weekday to help time promotions. Fridays and Saturdays run about 25% above midweek.

Holdout: predicted vs actual play intensity

18,788 machine-days, May–Jun. Points on the diagonal = perfect. The model beats the per-machine average baseline by 12% MAE — enough to separate "weak machine" from "weak spot".

Daily floor coin-in forecast, May–Jun

Weekday-seasonal model vs the constant training-mean baseline. The seasonal model cuts daily MAE from $218K to $69K — use it to time promotions and staffing to the weekend peak.

A-INSPECT ×1

Verify M-1158's paytable

$1,200 · 1-day lead · 120 min downtime
Effect
If the configured RTP is wrong and is reset to 10%, M-1158 recovers about $200/day — roughly $4.4K in July and $6K/month after.
Uncertainty
High variance in timing, low in size: the shortfall is 4.5σ below theory, but an inspection buys information, not a guaranteed finding.
Measure
Daily hold vs the 10% theo for 8 weeks, diff-in-diff against the 48 other Desert Gold machines.
Evidence
Section 04 — 3.0% hold vs 10% theo, all six months, peers at 7–14%.
A-MOVE-MACHINE ×3

Move M-1181, M-1265, M-1179 out of Back Forty

$5,400 · 2-day lead · 180 min downtime each
Effect
These three draw 20–24% less play than even Back Forty's average. Relocated to Main Entrance / Center Pit slots, the model projects ~$2.6K/month each in extra win (≈$7.8K/month total).
Uncertainty
Range $3–8K/month: part of each machine's low draw may be machine-specific and travel with it.
Measure
8-week diff-in-diff vs Back Forty Comet Cash controls (M-1063, M-1256) that stay put.
Evidence
Section 02 — zone play gap and location index 0.76–0.80.
A-REPLACE-LEASE ×2

Swap Royal Draw titles on M-1238 and M-1035

$6,760 · 14-day lead · 16 operating days each
Effect
Both draw above zone norms (t = +6.9, +5.9) but Royal Draw holds only 5%. A leased ~11%-hold title on the same footprint projects +$6–8K/month each if coin-in holds.
Uncertainty
Widest range of the three ($0–8K each): a new title may draw less play than the incumbent low-volatility game.
Measure
Play intensity and hold vs the three Royal Draw machines left as controls.
Evidence
Section 05 — game-mix table; Section 01 — play residuals.
Considered and deferred: Back Forty wayfinding signs ($7,500) — plausible zone-wide lift, but wait 8 weeks and let the three moves measure how much of the gap is placement; a technician shift ($11,520) — rejected because the bottom ten lost about $766 to downtime in six months; promotion weekend ($15,000) — floor demand is stable and seasonal, so the money is better spent where the diagnosis points.
